Personal Tax Returns

Whether you're a W-2 employee, a retiree, or filing for the first time, we prepare accurate federal and state returns that maximize your refund and minimize your stress.

  • Federal & State returns
  • All income types: wages, interest, dividends, Social Security
  • Deductions & credits reviewed thoroughly
  • E-filing for faster refunds

Business Tax Returns

Running a business is hard enough. Let us handle your business tax return with precision. We work with LLCs, S-Corporations, partnerships, and sole proprietors.

  • LLCs, S-Corps, C-Corps, Partnerships
  • Schedule C & Schedule K-1
  • Quarterly estimated taxes
  • Business deduction optimization

Self-Employed & 1099

Freelancers, independent contractors, and gig workers face unique tax situations. We know the deductions you're entitled to and make sure you're not paying more than you should.

  • 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-K filers
  • Home office, mileage & equipment deductions
  • Self-employment tax calculations
  • Quarterly estimated tax planning

ITIN Applications

We prepare and submit ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) applications for individuals who need to file taxes but are not eligible for a Social Security Number.

  • Form W-7 preparation & submission
  • Assistance for non-residents & resident aliens
  • ITIN renewals
✍

Amended Returns

Filed a return with an error? Changed your filing status? Forgot a deduction? We'll review your original return and file an amendment to set things right and recover what you're owed.

  • Form 1040-X preparation
  • Review of original return for errors
  • Recover missed credits & deductions
  • Prior year returns (up to 3 years)
📎

Notary Services

Our certified notary public is available in-office. Get your taxes and notarization done in the same visit — no extra trip required.

  • Legal documents & affidavits
  • Real estate & power of attorney documents
  • General notarization services
