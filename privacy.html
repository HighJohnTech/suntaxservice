Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: 2026

Sun Tax Service LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Tax-related information: Social Security Numbers (SSNs), Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers (ITINs), income information, financial statements, and other tax documentation provided during the preparation process.
  • Website usage data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, and time spent on our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Prepare, file, and amend your tax returns as requested.
  • Communicate with you about your tax situation, appointments, and account status.
  • Respond to your contact form submissions and inquiries.
  • Comply with IRS regulations, state tax laws, and other applicable legal requirements.
  • Improve the quality of our services.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All preparers at Sun Tax Service are IRS-registered and bound by professional confidentiality obligations.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal or financial information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose your information only:

  • With your express written consent.
  • As required by law, IRS regulation, or court order.
  • To government agencies (IRS, state tax authorities) as part of the tax filing process you have authorized.

5. IRS Preparer Notice

All tax returns prepared by Sun Tax Service are prepared by IRS-registered tax preparers holding valid Preparer Tax Identification Numbers (PTINs). Your tax preparer’s PTIN will appear on your completed return as required by IRS regulations.

6. Data Retention

We retain client tax records in accordance with IRS recordkeeping guidelines — generally a minimum of seven (7) years from the date of filing.

7. Contact Form Data

Information submitted through our contact form is transmitted securely and used solely to respond to your inquiry. It is not added to any marketing list without your consent.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements). Contact us at shawnagoodloe@gmail.com or (734) 483-5200.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
