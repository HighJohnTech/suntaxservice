Our Story

Most tax offices see you as a number. You walk in, hand over your documents, and leave with no idea what actually happened with your money.

That's not how we do it here.

Sun Tax Service was founded in 2008 by Shawna Goodloe a trained accounting professional with a degree in accounting and nearly two decades of experience helping real people navigate the tax system. What started in Ypsilanti has grown into a year-round, nationwide service but the approach has never changed.

We serve W-2 employees, small business owners, freelancers, gig workers, and anyone who needs an ITIN or notarization. Every client gets the same thing: personal attention, complete accuracy, and a team that genuinely cares.

“That's why we say: It's Different In Here.”
